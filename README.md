## About me

I am a first-year PhD student at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, supervised by [Eric Moulines](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/study/faculty/professor-eric-moulines/), [Maxim Panov](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/study/faculty/maxim-panov/), and [Alexander Korotin](https://akorotin.netlify.app/).

My research focuses on making powerful generative models faster, more practical, and easier to understand. I am especially interested in distillation methods for diffusion and matching models: how to preserve the behavior of a strong iterative teacher while replacing hundreds of sampling steps with just a few high-quality generation steps.

I like research that connects theory with systems people can actually run: efficient samplers, clean implementations, project pages, paper discussions, and tools that help more people enter the generative modeling conversation.

## Research interests

- Diffusion models and diffusion language models
- Distillation for fast generative inference
- Optimal transport, Schrodinger bridges, and matching models
- Discrete generative modeling for text
- Image restoration, super-resolution, and medical imaging
- Research communication and open-source ML tooling

## Selected work

| Project | Venue | What it is about | Links |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **IDLM: Inverse-distilled Diffusion Language Models** | ICML 2026 | Distills diffusion language models into few-step generators, targeting 4x-64x fewer inference steps while preserving generation quality. | [Project](https://david-cripto.github.io/idlm-project-page/) / [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19066) / [Code](https://github.com/David-cripto/IDLM) |
| **Universal Inverse Distillation for Matching Models with Real-Data Supervision** | ICLR 2026 Oral | A universal inverse distillation framework for matching models that uses real-data supervision without adversarial training. | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22459) / [Code](https://github.com/David-cripto/RealUID) |
| **Inverse Bridge Matching Distillation** | ICML 2025 | Distills diffusion bridge models for conditional and unconditional image-to-image translation, with reported 4x-100x acceleration. | [Paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=UCJSF6Vt0C) / [Code](https://github.com/ngushchin/IBMD) |
| **Diffusion and Adversarial Schrodinger Bridges via Iterative Proportional Markovian Fitting** | ICLR 2026 | Connects iterative Markovian fitting with iterative proportional fitting for solving Schrodinger bridge problems. | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02601) / [Code](https://github.com/gregkseno/ipmf) |
| **One-Step Residual Shifting Diffusion for Image Super-Resolution via Distillation** | ICML 2026 | Distills ResShift-style super-resolution diffusion into a one-step restoration model. | [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13358) |
